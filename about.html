About LocalAI Hardware

A site that summarizes published reviews of computers built for running AI models locally, and links to the original page of every review it summarizes.

Last updated 26 Oct 2026.
What changed: page added.

What this site is

LocalAI Hardware collects what published reviews say about computers built for running AI models locally.
Each product page shows the reviews source by source, with a link to every original page.

The site summarizes reviews.
It does not test products and does not score them.

How it is maintained

Every product page is re-checked each day: price, stock, new variants, new reviews, and whether each source link still opens.
A change is published only after it has been checked against the original page.

Each product page shows when it was last updated and what changed.
It also shows when its sources were last verified.

The full process is on the How we summarize reviews page.
