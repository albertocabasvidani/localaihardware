Apple Mac Studio (M5 Max and M5 Ultra)

Apple Mac Studio is a compact desktop with an M5 Max or M5 Ultra chip and up to 512 GB of unified memory, used by reviewers to run large local AI models and agents.

Last updated 29 Oct 2026.
What changed: M5 Ultra 512 GB unified memory option and price added.

Best for local AI agents on one quiet desk machineBest for large models with high memory bandwidthBest for pros who also edit photo and video

Labels drawn from what WIRED, MacStories, Tom's Hardware, Ars Technica, Digital Camera World say.

The short version
  • Praised: memory bandwidth and capacity for local models, a small and quiet body, and plenty of ports.
  • Criticized: the price of memory and SSD upgrades, heat and fan noise under load, and GPU speed behind NVIDIA.
  • Speed: Ars Technica measured just over 50 tokens/sec on a 27B model with the M5 Ultra and about 31 with the M5 Max.
  • Suits: people who want large models and local agents on one desktop. Ars Technica calls it a "Niche product for a small audience".

Pros extracted from reviews, with who said it.

Flaws but not dealbreakers

Cons extracted from reviews, with who said it.

  • –Expensive, especially as you add memory and SSD Mentioned by Tom's Hardware, Ars Technica, Digital Camera World, WIRED ("cheaper AI boxes out there"), Engadget
  • –Memory and SSD can't be upgraded after purchase Mentioned by Tom's Hardware
  • –Can get hot and fans can be heard under heavy load Mentioned by WIRED, Tom's Hardware

  • –Slow for video generation: over 11 minutes for a 15-second clip Mentioned by WIRED
  • –A niche product for a small audience Mentioned by Ars Technica

Noise, power and heat

Measured or heard by the sources, with the source named.

  • Fans: "Quiet fans" (Digital Camera World). MacStories found it "appreciably quieter" than an RTX 5090 PC. WIRED: the fans can spin up quite loudly.
  • Heat: WIRED lists "Can get very hot". Tom's Hardware measured an average CPU temperature of 78.29 °C in its stress test.
  • Power: About 75 W on average in a video transcode (Ars Technica). Apple states 480 W maximum continuous power (Apple specifications).

Who it is for

What reviewers say about the buyer.

  • Suits: Local AI agents: "a dream machine for local AI agents" (MacStories).
  • Suits: Local coding models and other local ML work (Ars Technica).
  • Suits: Photographers and video shooters who also run AI tools (Digital Camera World).
  • Less suited: Buyers on a budget: WIRED lists "There are cheaper AI boxes out there"; Ars Technica calls it a "Niche product for a small audience".

Where reviews disagree

Differences between sources, side by side.

  • AI strength: MacStories (M5 Ultra) calls it a dream machine for local agents. Digital Camera World (M5 Max) says it is less powerful than a GeForce 5070Ti desktop card in AI benchmarks.
  • Speed against NVIDIA: Tom's Hardware found prompt processing faster than the DGX Spark. MacStories measured an RTX 5090 PC reading prompts faster: 3,031 against 1,701 tokens/sec.
  • Heat: WIRED: "Can get very hot". MacStories: warm to the touch.

Alternatives

Other products with their own pages on this site.
