AMD Ryzen AI Halo

AMD Ryzen AI Halo is a compact mini PC built around the Ryzen AI Max+ 395 chip with 128 GB of unified memory, made for running and developing local AI models on Windows or Linux.

Last updated 31 Oct 2026.
What changed: TOPC TXA2 and Framework Desktop (192 GB) variants added; GMKtec EVO-X5 Pro now in stock.

Best for running large models in 128 GBBest for a turn-key local AI setup on Windows or LinuxBest for open-source software stacks

Labels drawn from what TechPowerUp, Tom's Hardware, Phoronix, XDA, StorageReview, Signal65 say.

The short version
  • Praised: 128 GB of memory, a compact box that is ready to use, and both Windows and Linux.
  • Criticized: the price, slower prompt processing than the DGX Spark, and audible fans.
  • Speed: Signal65 measured token generation close to the Spark. Prompt processing was slower in every test.
  • Suits: developers who want 128 GB with AMD software. StorageReview sends buyers who want maximum tokens per second to the Spark.

Noise, power and heat

Measured or heard by the sources, with the source named.

  • Idle power: 12 W, against more than 20 W for the DGX Spark (TechPowerUp).
  • Peak power: 83 W from the wall (XDA); about 196 W under load (Signal65).
  • Fans: Tom's Hardware heard the twin blower fans "audibly even at idle" with a "notable high-pitched whine". TechPowerUp lists "No fan-stop in idle, a bit loud under load".
  • Temperature: At or below the mid-50s °C in Tom's Hardware.

Who it is for

What reviewers say about the buyer.

  • Suits: AI developers who are not tied to CUDA: "This is a great prototyping box for AI developers who aren't wedded to CUDA" (XDA).
  • Suits: ROCm developers and teams that need Windows (StorageReview).
  • Suits: Buyers who want an open-source software stack (Phoronix).
  • Less suited: Buyers who need maximum tokens per second or plan to cluster nodes (StorageReview).

Where reviews disagree

Differences between sources, side by side.

  • Price against the Spark: TechPowerUp calls the Halo "Much cheaper than NVIDIA DGX Spark (which is $7,000 now)". Tom's Hardware says it is "not much cheaper than those systems". StorageReview says "the halo is at parity" against the category baseline.
  • Speed against the Spark: Signal65 measured token generation within 10% of the Spark on all five models. StorageReview measured the Spark ahead in every vLLM test at batch 64.
  • Noise: Signal65: "Both machines ran cool and quiet throughout our testing." Tom's Hardware heard the fans at idle.

Alternatives

Other products with their own pages on this site.

  • NVIDIA DGX Spark: Reviewers found its prompt processing faster than the Halo's. See the NVIDIA DGX Spark page.
  • Apple Mac Studio: M5 Max and M5 Ultra desktops, reviewed for local AI. See the Apple Mac Studio page.
  • NVIDIA DGX Station: A desk-side workstation with 748 GB of memory. See the NVIDIA DGX Station page.

What each source says

Every judgment is labeled by its source, so you can form your own view.

TechPowerUp
Review siteReview: "AMD Ryzen AI Halo Review - Local-AI Powerhouse with 128 GB of Memory"

AMD's Ryzen AI Halo Mini-PC is branded a "Developer Platform," but it really has the potential to be a personal AI appliance for power users running big models locally.

Praises 128 GB of memory, 10 Gbit Ethernet and low idle power; criticizes the $4K price, slower time to first token than the DGX Spark and 256 GB/s bandwidth.

Positive
Tom's Hardware
Review siteReview: "AMD Ryzen AI Halo review: AMD builds a DGX Spark of its own"

The Ryzen AI Halo lives up to its promise as a turn-key local AI platform within AMD's AI ecosystem…

Likes the easy setup and software; says performance trails the DGX Spark and GB10 boxes and it is "not much cheaper than those systems".

Mixed
XDA
Review site⚑ AMD provided the review unitReview: "AMD's tiny Ryzen AI box does what Nvidia's DGX Spark does at a fraction of the power"

The AMD Ryzen AI Halo is just as easy to recommend as the DGX Spark to the same audience.

Scores it 9/10 and calls it a great prototyping box for AI developers who are not tied to CUDA; the Spark is about 5x faster at prefill.

Positive
Phoronix

Positive
