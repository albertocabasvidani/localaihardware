NVIDIA DGX Station (GB300)

NVIDIA DGX Station is a desk-side AI workstation built around the GB300 Grace Blackwell Ultra chip with 748 GB of coherent memory, sold through system makers such as ASUS, HP and MSI.

Last updated 27 Oct 2026.
What changed: MSI XpertStation WS300 variant added.

Best for very large models on one deskBest for high-throughput local inference for teamsBest for linking two units over 400G

Labels drawn from what StorageReview (MSI), StorageReview (ASUS), StorageReview (two-Station cluster), ServeTheHome say.

The short version
  • Praised: memory for very large models, throughput far above the DGX Spark and an RTX PRO 6000, and carry handles on the ASUS tower.
  • Criticized: the price, the 20A power circuit, the Arm host and the missing display output without an add-in card.
  • Speed: StorageReview (MSI) measured 9,294 output tokens/sec on GPT-OSS-120B at 128 streams, against 500 for the DGX Spark.
  • Suits: teams that need frontier-scale inference at a desk. StorageReview (MSI): "Outside that niche, the argument for buying these systems is pretty limited."

Noise, power and heat

Measured or heard by the sources, with the source named.

  • Noise: 37–40 dBA at lower loads and 46–49 dBA at full load (ServeTheHome).
  • Power: Idle never below 250 W (ServeTheHome). The GPU drew 1,292 W in a test (StorageReview (MSI)); system limit is 1,600 W (NVIDIA).
  • Heat: B300 peaked at 71 °C and the CPU at 65 °C (StorageReview (MSI)).

Who it is for

What reviewers say about the buyer.

  • Suits: Teams that need frontier-scale models and shared inference at a desk (StorageReview (MSI)).
  • Suits: Buyers who want to add a second Station later: two Stations linked at near line rate (StorageReview (two-Station cluster)).
  • Less suited: Most other buyers: "Outside that niche, the argument for buying these systems is pretty limited" (StorageReview (MSI)).

Where reviews disagree

Differences between sources, side by side.

  • Verdicts: All four sources are positive. The disagreement is about cost: StorageReview (MSI) says "an RTX PRO 6000 workstation costs far less", while StorageReview (two-Station cluster) says the bar is set "price aside".
